Profitability matrix

Revenue capture (billed charges as a share of gross potential rent) against physical occupancy. The source workbooks contain no expense data, so revenue capture is a revenue-efficiency proxy, not NOI. Dot size is unit count. Revenue capture already embeds occupancy, so a property far to the left of its occupancy level is losing money to pricing, concessions or collections rather than to vacancy - that gap is the diagnostic. Click a dot to open the property; where dots overlap you will be asked which one you meant.
